# Thai administrative boundaries for ARutGIS

`rtsd/adm1.geojson` (provinces), `adm2` (districts) and `adm3` (subdistricts): the
Royal Thai Survey Department's (กรมแผนที่ทหาร) administrative boundaries, published by
OCHA ROAP on the Humanitarian Data Exchange as "Thailand - Subnational Administrative
Boundaries" (https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-tha), licensed CC BY-IGO
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/).

Converted to GeoJSON (WGS 84) and simplified by about 30 m with GDAL's ogr2ogr by
.github/workflows/thai-boundaries-data.yml. For mapping and analysis, not legal
boundaries.
